Point-of-interest (POI) recommendation models based on graph neural networks achieve strong performance by propagating collaborative signals over user-item interactions, yet they struggle with the cold-start problem, where items with few or no interactions are not represented. In this paper, we propose LLM-augmented Multi-Graph Contrastive Learning (LLM-MGCL), a multi-graph neural network that uses semantic and spatial information about items to extend the LightGCN backbone with two auxiliary item-item graphs: a semantic graph constructed from sentence embeddings of LLM-generated photo summari
