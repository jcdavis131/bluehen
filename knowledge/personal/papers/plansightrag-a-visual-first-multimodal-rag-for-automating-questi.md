Civil infrastructure compliance checking has long relied on engineers manually reading legacy 2D plans; however, OCR-based automation strips away the geometry and layout essential for interpreting these plans. We present a Visual-First Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) framework called PlanSightRAG. It indexes and reasons directly over plan imagery, integrates a ColNomic-3B multi-vector retrieval, an agentic Planner-Retriever-Auditor-Synthesizer, and MaxSim heatmaps as an evidence trail.
