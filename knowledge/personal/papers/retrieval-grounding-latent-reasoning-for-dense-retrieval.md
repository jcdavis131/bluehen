Reasoning-intensive retrieval requires text representations to capture not only semantic similarity, but also the reasoning needed to determine relevance under a given retrieval instruction. Existing reasoning-enhanced embedding models improve retrieval by incorporating reasoning information into dense representations, yet their supervision is typically dominated by the final retrieval objective.
