Enterprises connect language models to their own data through retrieval. The benchmarks that rank multi-hop retrieval systems leave out two facts a buyer needs before a published number can be used: whether the retrieval backbone may be deployed commercially, and what it costs to build. On licensing: the field's dense-retrieval anchor, NV-Embed-v2, is licensed cc-by-nc-4.0.
