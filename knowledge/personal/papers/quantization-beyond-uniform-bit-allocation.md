Quantization is a fundamental technique to handle the growing sizes of embeddings generated by modern models. Existing quantization schemes are largely embedding agnostic and allocate bits uniformly across dimensions. However, recent models produce embeddings with significant geometric structure. In this work, we investigate whether a variable bit allocation scheme can improve quantization quality under a fixed memory budget.
